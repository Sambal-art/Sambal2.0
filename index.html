Brand identity

Silver Spoon Bespoke Tiling

A premium identity in silver and blue, built on negative-space tile imagery.

Silver SpoonBrand kitSilver SpoonLogo variationsSilver SpoonMockups
Read the story: from just another local tiler to a premium brand
Before

Skilled, new, and invisible.

He had the skill and a brand-new tiling business. What he did not have was a face. No brand, no presence, nothing for a customer to remember.

To most people who found him, he was one more local tile installer, so they treated him like one: they haggled over the price. The few customers he did win were the least ready to pay well and the most ready to ask for more.

The customers who gladly pay a premium for tiling done properly never saw him. They could not choose a man they could not see.

What we did

He asked for a flyer. We built him a brand.

A flyer would only have told people what he does. His real problem was that nobody knew who he was. So we built a full brand identity, and we started with his story, because a story is the one thing a competitor cannot copy.

The quality of what people see online decides the quality of the clients who come.

The story decided the logo. The logo decided the colours. The colours decided how everything else looks and speaks. What we delivered:

  • A brand kit with the guidelines, his story and the colour palette
  • The logo in its colour variations
  • Business cards in two variations
  • Mockups of the brand in the real world
  • eFlyers and ad creatives
After

A position no one else can copy.

The brand is freshly delivered, so there are no sales figures yet, and we would rather show none than invent any.

What has already changed is the man behind it. He now stands behind a brand that says who he really is. He has more confidence, a clear sense of mission, and clarity on where the business is going and how it will serve its market.

The result is a brand with a unique, exclusive place in the marketplace. His own words and the first results will be added here as they come in.
